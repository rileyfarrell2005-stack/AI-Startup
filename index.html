Help & information

Settings and customer help

A few important details about this FixerUpper prototype.

How do I request a repair?

Filter technicians, open a profile, and submit a quote request. Choose an illustrative open date and preferred time; the technician must confirm directly.

How do quotes, fees and appointments work?

Quote requests and browsing are free. Before accepting a fixed-price quote, customers see the repair price, the separate $5 convenience fee and the total. If the repair is marked complete, the demo simulates that customer fee and a separate $5 technician platform fee; the technician's estimated earnings are the repair price minus that fee. Declines and incomplete repairs incur no fees.

Are profiles and availability real?

No. Sample profiles, ratings, reviews and available dates are fictional demo data.

Are these real charges or payments?

No. This is a fictional university prototype. Fees, totals and technician earnings are demonstrations only; the app does not process payments or collect financial information.

Where is demo data stored?

Requests, quote replies, ratings and simulated completion records are stored in local browser storage on this device. They do not sync across devices and are not sent to a real service.
