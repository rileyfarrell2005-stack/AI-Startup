A little about us

Repair should feel more human.

FixerUpper is a student project exploring a friendlier way for people who need an appliance fixed to connect with independent repair technicians.

Built around clear communication

Customers can describe a problem in one place. Technicians can respond with questions and estimates, so both sides can decide what works before arranging a repair.

This website is a fictional student prototype, not a real repair marketplace. Profiles, posts, estimates, and messages are examples only.
