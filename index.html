Help & information

Settings and customer help

A few important details about using this FixerUpper prototype.

How do I request a repair?

Choose “Request services,” filter technicians by specialty and availability, open a profile, and send a quote request describing the appliance problem.

How do quotes and booking work?

The technician replies with a price estimate and message. Review it in “My repair requests.” You can accept or decline. Contact and visit arrangements are made directly between the customer and technician.

Does FixerUpper visit my home?

No. FixerUpper is a connection platform concept only. The customer and independent service provider communicate and make arrangements directly. Always verify provider qualifications and agree on scope and price before work begins.

Are profiles and reviews real?

No. All sample profiles, ratings, availability, requests, messages, and reviews are fictional demonstration content. Customer reviews posted in this demo stay in this browser.

Where is my demo data stored?

Requests, quote replies, decisions, ratings, and reviews are stored in local browser storage on this device. This is not a real account system and information is not shared with other people or devices.

How do payment options work?

Payment choices shown after accepting a quote are a visual prototype only. This site does not connect to banks, send Interac e-Transfers, or process Apple Pay. Never enter bank passwords or card details into this demo.
